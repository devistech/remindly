# Privacy Policy for Remindly

**Last updated: June 1, 2026**

## Introduction

Remindly ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application Remindly (the "Service").

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
Remindly stores all your reminder data **locally on your device** using Hive (a local database). This includes:
- Reminder titles and descriptions
- Category selections
- Date and time information
- Repeat settings and notification preferences
- Language preference

**We do NOT collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on our servers.** All data remains entirely on your device.

### Information Collected by Third Parties

#### Google AdMob (Advertising)
Our app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and use data as described in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). This may include:
- Device identifiers (advertising ID)
- Device type and operating system version
- IP address
- Ad interaction data

You can opt out of personalized advertising by:
- Going to **Settings > Google > Ads** on your Android device
- Tapping "Opt out of Ads Personalization"

## Permissions We Use

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `INTERNET` | Required for loading advertisements via Google AdMob |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Required to send reminder notifications (Android 13+) |
| `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` | Required to schedule precise reminder notifications |
| `USE_EXACT_ALARM` | Required for exact alarm scheduling (Android 12+) |
| `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` | Required to reschedule reminders after device restart |
| `VIBRATE` | Required for notification vibration |
| `WAKE_LOCK` | Required to ensure notifications are delivered on time |

## Data Security

- All reminder data is stored locally on your device using Hive, an encrypted local database
- No data is transmitted to external servers
- We do not use analytics or tracking services
- We do not sell, trade, or transfer your information to third parties

## Children's Privacy

Remindly is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.

## Your Rights

Since we do not collect or store any personal data on our servers, there is no personal data for us to access, modify, or delete. All your reminder data is stored locally on your device and can be managed entirely through the app's interface. You can delete all your data at any time by using the "Clear All Data" option in the app's Settings.
