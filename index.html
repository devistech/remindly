Introduction

Remindly ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application Remindly (the "Service").

We believe in transparency and want you to understand exactly what data our app accesses, how it is used, and your rights regarding your information.

📄

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

Remindly stores all your reminder data locally on your device using Hive (a local database). This includes:

  • Reminder titles and descriptions
  • Category selections
  • Date and time information
  • Repeat settings and notification preferences
  • Language preference

We do NOT collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on our servers. All data remains entirely on your device.

Information Collected by Third Parties

Our app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and use data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. This may include:

  • Device identifiers (advertising ID)
  • Device type and operating system version
  • IP address
  • Ad interaction data

You can opt out of personalized advertising by going to Settings > Google > Ads on your Android device and tapping "Opt out of Ads Personalization".

💾

Data Storage

All reminder data is stored locally on your device using Hive, a lightweight and fast database built for Flutter. The data stored includes:

  • Reminder titles and descriptions
  • Scheduled dates and times
  • Category assignments
  • Language preferences
  • App settings

This data is never shared with third parties and never leaves your device.

🔔

Notifications

Remindly uses local notifications to remind you about your scheduled tasks. These notifications are generated entirely on your device. No notification data is sent to or received from external servers.

📺

Advertising

Remindly displays advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use data for advertising purposes in accordance with Google's privacy policy.

You can learn more about Google's data practices at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

To opt out of personalized ads, navigate to your device's Settings > Google > Ads and disable ad personalization.

🔐

Permissions We Use

Remindly requests only the permissions necessary for its core functionality:

Permission Purpose
INTERNET Loading advertisements via Google AdMob
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Sending reminder notifications (Android 13+)
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM Scheduling precise reminder notifications
USE_EXACT_ALARM Exact alarm scheduling (Android 12+)
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Rescheduling reminders after device restart
VIBRATE Notification vibration
WAKE_LOCK Ensuring notifications are delivered on time
🛡

Data Security

✓ All data stored locally using Hive, an encrypted local database
✓ No data transmitted to external servers
✓ No analytics or tracking services
✓ We do not sell, trade, or transfer your information
🗑

Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can delete all your data at any time by going to Settings > Clear All Data within the app. This will permanently remove all reminders and settings from your device.

👶

Children's Privacy

Remindly is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

⚖

Your Rights

Since we do not collect or store any personal data on our servers, there is no personal data for us to access, modify, or delete. All your reminder data is stored locally on your device and can be managed entirely through the app's interface.

You can delete all your data at any time by using the "Clear All Data" option in the app's Settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.
